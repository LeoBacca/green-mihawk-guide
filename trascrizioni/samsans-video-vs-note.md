## 4. Disagreements / corrections

1. **Boat reasoning is inverted.** note.txt (Boats): "Shanks and other expensive middle-game cards naturally use more DON!!. There are fewer turns where the deck simply has spare DON!! to convert an event." Sam at **00:21:34**: "without the more expensive cards in the middle of your game like the Shankses, the seven and nine, it is just very easy for you to have DON open for you to convert the Rad Beams" — i.e. the 7c/9c Shanks are gone, so there is *more* free DON and Boat's DON-cheating is less needed; the fewer-events point is the second reason.

2. **Decken and Kaido.** note.txt (Kaido, Matchup Identity): "Decken has made the matchup worse than it was previously" listed under why Kaido is dangerous for Mihawk. Sam at **00:17:37**: "the biggest reason why I would not play Kaido is that the Decken makes the matchup so much worse than it was before. Without it for sure you will lose to Kaido" — Decken made the matchup worse *for Kaido*.

3. **Kaido: pick first.** note.txt never mentions it; Sam at **01:37:39**: "Kaido is the only matchup I can think of where you want to pick first." (Everywhere else she takes second; vs Sabo she is ~60% second.)

4. **Kaido tone.** note.txt calls it "reasonable but more difficult than Rocks or yellow" and says Kaido "can make the standard Shanks plus Vander Decken plan less effective" and lists "A plan for whether Shanks or Law/Bepo is the better first bomb". Sam (**01:36:52–01:43:15**) says there's "not a ton of interesting stuff to do", "it's pretty chill", "if they miss the King you always win", never discusses which bomb to play first, and says the Shanks 12 is uncoverable at their second bomb.

5. **What stops King.** note.txt: "Perona or other stuns to stop King." Sam **01:42:27**: it is specifically Oden on 7 and 9, because "none of the other fives you can play, especially with a Mihawk, actually do anything" there.

6. **Luna vs Kaido.** note.txt does not mention it; Sam **01:41:39**: Luna is "very uniquely bad" in that matchup, generally pitched.

7. **"Early 8k attacks" attributed to Kaido.** note.txt (Kaido, Key Priorities): "Enough defense to avoid early 8k attacks." In the video the "8k swings very early in the game" line is at **01:54:02**, inside the RG Luffy Ace answer, not Kaido.

8. **Bomb count "four" vs "five".** note.txt (Law/Bepo Count): "She does not believe four copies are necessary." Sam **00:29:23**: "I just don't think that you need five" (context ambiguous — likely total bombs). She never says "four".

9. **Brook.** note.txt (Sabo): "Sabo can use Giant lines, Saul, Loki, Luffy, and Brook to produce too many attacks." Brook is never mentioned in the video. Cards actually named for Sabo: Saul, Loki, Gravity Blade, Docking (Station), 8c Luffy, Zoro, Jinbe blocker, 2-drops, 12-costs.

10. **Perona vs Rocks.** note.txt has a "Perona is excellent [vs Rocks]" key-card block. Perona is not mentioned once in the Rocks segment (01:26:24–01:36:04); the cards she credits there are standing 6+5 boards, Rad Beam (vs Shiki), Shanks+Luna, Decken (on Shiki), and Stun Bonney off the 10.

11. **Ace Luffy section over-states.** note.txt: "Sam views Ace Luffy as generally favorable... Ace Luffy often has limited counter... Push life because Ace Luffy often cannot defend repeated 7k attacks." Sam **01:52:26**: "I don't have a great answer for this"; favoured going second, going first "maybe problematic". The supporting fact she does give (00:36:24) is that the deck "has no 2ks".

12. **Sections with no basis in the video.** "Search Priority" (four per-matchup priority lists) and "Know Which Attack Matters" under General Mihawk Principles are not said anywhere in the 2 hours; they are the note-writer's synthesis. Same for "Mihawk can attack with a 12k threat on the same turn... The opponent is often pushed to zero life while their board is disabled" phrased as a numbered Shanks-turn recipe — the pieces are in the video, the checklist form is not.

13. **"65-body" (Going Second).** note.txt: "Develop a 65-body on 6. Develop another 65-body on 8." Sam (**00:43:34, 00:54:48**) says "a six drop on six and then a six drop on eight"; "you see five, they see 65" (01:01:25 questioner) means five-drop / six+five. "65-body" looks like a transcription artefact.

14. **Sabo: what Perona does when poked.** note.txt (Yasopp Versus Perona): Perona "protects against removal by replacing itself". Sam **01:07:10**: "the Perona will float into something if it dies, so you can just let it die and summon an Oden" — same idea, but the mechanism is that its death triggers another summon, not that it protects itself.

15. **Robin / Bonney timing.** note.txt (Stun Bonney Role) implies Bonney on the opponent's first Mom turn generically; Sam adds the important caveat (**01:46:29**) that vs a triple-ramping Robin that means Bonney on *your six*, which costs you the Mihawk turn — acceptable but not ideal.

16. **Leader size.** note.txt never gives it; the transcript itself is inconsistent ("7k leader" at 01:17:37 vs "6k leader against them" at 01:29:35). Flagging so the guide checks the actual card.
